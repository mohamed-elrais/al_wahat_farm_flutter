Al Wahat Farm — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 August 2026

Al Wahat Farm ("the app") is an internal farm-operations application operated by Macaws Hub OU for the Al Wahat farm. Access requires an account created by the farm's administrators; the app is not intended for general public use.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, used to sign you in and identify your work inside the farm team.
  • Work records — tasks, schedules, inventory movements, reviews, and notes you create or complete in the app.
  • Photos — evidence photos you attach to tasks or issue reports. Photos are taken only when you choose to attach them.

What we do not collect

  • No location tracking. The farm map is static geometry bundled with the app; your device's GPS is not used.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no sale of data.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How your information is used

Data is used solely to run the farm's operations: assigning and reviewing work, planning irrigation and fertilization, and keeping inventory accurate. It is visible only to authorized members of the farm team according to their role.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed Supabase (PostgreSQL) backend and transmitted exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections. Evidence photos are stored in a private storage bucket accessible only to authorized team members through time-limited links. Some data is cached on your device to keep the app usable offline and is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Data retention and deletion

Operational records are retained for as long as the farm requires them. To correct or delete your account or its data, contact the farm administrator or email us at the address below; accounts are deactivated and personal data removed on request.

Children

The app is a workplace tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and significant changes will be communicated inside the app or by email.
